Food information standards

  • Ingredients and allergen statements must be confirmed against the approved recipe.
  • Nutrition values and health claims must not be invented or inferred from a product name.
  • Jain-friendly, satvik and other dietary claims require explicit operational approval.
  • Recipe changes should update current product information while preserving historical order snapshots.

Help & answers

Frequently asked questions

Is this a live ordering website?

No. This is a private UAT prototype with sample data. Checkout cannot create a real order.

When is delivery planned?

The Phase 1 operating model has a planned 7–9 PM delivery window.

Are prices and offers final?

No. All values in this prototype are sample values used to test the pricing and offer logic.

Are all products available every day?

Availability is a separate control from product lifecycle and website visibility. The live system must show only approved, published and currently orderable items.

How do I ask about ingredients or allergens?

Contact Purefolk directly. Product claims and allergen information must be confirmed from approved recipe data.

Important separations

  • Product lifecycle ≠ website visibility ≠ availability ≠ ordering state.
  • Product ID ≠ SKU. Variant / sellable configuration remains OPEN.
  • MRP and base selling price are manual business inputs. Discounts and final payable amount are derived.
  • Website is a customer interface. Nucleus is the source of truth.
  • Order confirmation must snapshot product name, configuration, price and offer at purchase time.
