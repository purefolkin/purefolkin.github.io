The small details

Good to know.

A few clear answers before you explore the demo menu.

UAT PREVIEW
Can I place a real order here?

No. This is an isolated website prototype. You can add demo items to the cart and change quantities, but checkout is deliberately disabled. No order, payment, message or customer record is created.

Are these the final prices?

No. All displayed prices are sample values used to make the prototype feel realistic. They are not a live offer or approved commercial price list.

What does the 7–9 PM window mean?

It reflects Purefolk’s planned Phase 1 delivery window. Actual service areas, order cut-offs, capacity and availability need to be confirmed before ordering is enabled.

Are every recipe and dietary label confirmed?

No. Product descriptions and labels are demonstration content. Recipes, ingredients, allergens, nutrition values and Jain/satvik claims must be checked and approved before launch.
