State boundaries

✓
LifecycleDraft, review, approved, archived—whether the record exists and is valid.
▧
PublicationUnpublished, scheduled, published—whether content is visible on the website.
◈
Availability / orderabilityWhether an item can be ordered now; not the same as publication.
₹
PaymentPending, paid, failed, refunded or partially refunded—separate from order fulfilment.
⌁
FulfilmentPreparation and delivery states with controlled transitions and exception handling.

Release safeguards

1
Stable IDsNames can change; product, variant, recipe and order identities remain stable.
2
Historical snapshotsPast orders preserve purchased names, configuration and price even after catalogue edits.
3
Validation and auditTrack who changed what, why, when and whether the change was approved.
4
Controlled publicationEdits do not silently publish. Validate and approve before the website changes.
